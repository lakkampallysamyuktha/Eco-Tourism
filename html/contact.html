Before You Travel With Us

What to expect, honestly

A short, straight account of how planning works — and what we won't promise.

  1. 01

    We start with a conversation

    Not a booking form. Your first message goes to a person who plans routes, not to a sales team.

  2. 02

    Timing decides the trip

    We plan around seasons, water and wildlife windows — not around when you happen to be free. Sometimes the answer is "come next year."

  3. 03

    Small groups only

    Group size is capped at twelve — on every departure, in every region. No exceptions, even for larger requests.

  4. 04

    We'll be honest about limits

    If a region can't comfortably host your dates, we'll say so. If we're not the right fit, we'll say that too.

  5. 05

    No unsupported claims

    We don't sell "carbon neutral" trips, "guaranteed sightings" or "off-the-beaten-path" experiences that are on every itinerary.
